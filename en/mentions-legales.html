Privacy

This site collects no personal data: no form, no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracker, and no third-party service loaded in the page. Fonts are hosted on the site itself.

Only your language choice (French or English) is remembered, so that the right version is shown on your next visit: in your browser’s local storage and in a technical cookie named lang (value “fr” or “en”, kept for 12 months), read by the page to show you the right language straight away. This cookie only serves to respect a choice you made and is exempt from consent (article 82 of the French Data Protection Act, CNIL guidelines). You can delete it at any time by clearing the site’s data in your browser.

Like any host, GitHub (and Cloudflare, which delivers the pages) technically logs requests (IP address, date, requested page) to keep the service secure and working; the publisher does not use these logs.

If you write to the contact address, your message and address are used only to reply to you and are not passed on to third parties.

Links to YouTube, GitHub and the project sites open external services, which have their own privacy policies.
